Form follows stillness.

Before sketching a single wall, we study the sun’s trajectory across the topography. True architecture does not impose upon nature; it gathers light, shadows, and breezes to cultivate an atmosphere of profound serenity.

NATURAL LIGHTING • THERMAL HARMONY
02 / 03

Materiality that ages with grace.

We reject transient novelties in favor of tactile honesty. Board-marked concrete, bush-hammered limestone, hand-rubbed smoked oak, and cast bronze develop a noble patina over generations, deepening the house’s emotional presence.

ENDURING TACTILITY • LOCAL CRAFTSMANSHIP

Living between inside and sky.

PHILOSOPHICAL ANCHOR

Architecture is more than a structure. It is a feeling.

Founded on the belief that environment dictates wellbeing, ATELIER bridges visionary architecture with bespoke interior architecture. We conceive each commission as a singular holistic artwork.

From cliffside pavilions perched over maritime horizons to secluded alpine sanctuaries, our design method begins with exhaustive site listening. We balance bold geometric cantilevers with intimate, human-scale sanctuaries where daylight paints silent poetry.

INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE

Less, but meaningful.

Interior design at ATELIER is not decoration applied after the fact. It is the continuation of architectural volume inward. We select honest, tactile materials that engage the human senses—natural light pouring over bush-hammered limestone, the subtle scent of smoked oak, and the gentle drape of raw Belgian linen.

“Simplicity is not a lack of clutter, but a mastery of proportion.” — ATELIER Materiality Thesis

The Tactile Palette

Touch, light reflection, and natural aging determine every material in our specification library.

01

Roman Travertine

Warm honey-hued sedimentary stone with soft cross-cut veining. Naturally cool underfoot and endlessly durable.

02

Smoked European Oak

Slow-fumed continuous rift-sawn oak finished with natural plant oils for deep, matte warmth without synthetic gloss.

03

Raw Belgian Linen

Unbleached heavyweight flax woven by heritage mills in Flanders, filtering sharp sun into diffuse ambient luminescence.

04

Cast Bronze & Glass

Hand-finished gunmetal bronze door hardware and fluted architectural partitions that create subtle acoustic dampening.

OUR PRACTICE & ETHOS

Good design starts with listening.

We collaborate with discerning clients who seek homes of enduring character rather than fleeting architectural fashions. Every design emerges from an intimate dialogue with the client’s daily rituals, the micro-climate, and the topography.

Operating studios in Zurich and New York, our international team brings together registered architects, interior designers, landscape masters, and structural specialists under one unified vision.

01

Listen & Envision

Site immersion, solar analysis, topographic profiling, and in-depth lifestyle discovery.

02

Form & Materiality

Hand sketches, 3D parametric iterations, material sampling, and acoustic modeling.

03

Technical Precision

Comprehensive engineering coordination, sustainable envelope detailing, and BIM modeling.

04

Living Craft

Rigorous on-site artisan supervision, custom furniture curation, and seamless handover.
